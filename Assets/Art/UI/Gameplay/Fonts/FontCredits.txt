The Secret Font of Monkey Island 1991, refined
Source: https://www.ptless.org/sfomi/
Imported from the user-provided MonkeyIsland-1991-refined.ttf.
Monkey Island 2: LeChuck's Revenge font copyright 1991 LucasArts.
Applied to scientist dialogue text at 16px (compact) and 24px (ending).
The author recommends pixel sizes divisible by 8. The optional historical
vertical scale of 1.2 is intended for sizes of at least 40px; it is not applied
to this 640x360 HUD. Non-integer display scaling can still soften the pixels.
