# Stable crate flotation

Effective weight is continuous: `dryWeight * Lerp(1, fullSubmersionWeightMultiplier, submergedFraction)`. The same calculation is used by push strength/stack weight and flotation. Full Submersion Weight Multiplier is configured in PhysicsSettings and defaults to 0.5. The push threshold belongs to each animal (Player Movement > Movement Settings > Maximum Push Weight), defaulting to effective stack weight >= 5. At half immersion a unit crate weighs 0.75; at full immersion it weighs 0.5.

PropsMotion registers active instances and groups vertically supported crates before any crate's individual physics tick. Groups touching water are simulated together using PhysicsSettings.Simulation Substeps per fixed tick (default 4). Total vertical force sums each member's buoyancy minus its effective weight times crate gravity, plus existing passenger loads, exactly once. Total dry mass determines acceleration. The solver starts from mass-weighted vertical momentum and applies a common vertical velocity; individual ticks keep horizontal drag, movement and carrying, but skip a second vertical integration. Crates rejoin/separate according to current support contacts on the next fixed tick.

Critical damping is estimated from the waterline stiffness (buoyancy plus the progressive gravity reduction, divided by collider height) and total dry mass. Configured water drag is retained if stronger. PropsBuoyancy.Vertical Damping Ratio defaults to 1. Stacks enforce at least critical damping; increasing this value produces slower settling. Fully submerged stacks use water drag because no member intersects the waterline.

SimulatedBody2D.Max Speed on migrated prop prefabs is **2 world units/s** (64 source pixels/s at 32 PPU). Both upward and downward velocities are capped. Stacks use the lowest member limit; swimming pushes are clamped and all movement/correction calls share a per-fixed-tick total travel budget, avoiding repeated corrections exceeding the cap. Dry upper crates carried by a water stack share this cap. The same body cap applies outside water, including free falls and balloon motion.

The common swept vertical clearance ignores group members and respects external solids, rope reach and player headroom. Leading members move first. A player's accumulated carry is included in clearance queries because PlayerPlatformRide applies it after the crate ticks. Contact topology changes are rebuilt on the next tick rather than recursively integrating the same load multiple times.

For a single unit crate with lift 100 and gravity 66, balance solves `100*f = 66*(1 - 0.5*f)`, giving `f = 66/133`, approximately 49.6% immersion. A stack's equilibrium depends on each member's current immersion and buoyancy settings; it can sink if total lift cannot balance its load. Side-by-side touching crates are not a vertical stack unless one supports the other. Support grouping follows the same down-cast contact model used by existing crate carrying, rather than distributing loads across multiple independent bridges.
